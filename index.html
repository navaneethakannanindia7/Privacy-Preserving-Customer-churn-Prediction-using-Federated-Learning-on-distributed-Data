Key Findings

  • Federated Learning achieves comparable accuracy to centralized training while preserving data privacy
  • The global model benefits from diverse data patterns across distributed clients
  • Month-to-month contracts and high monthly charges are strong churn indicators
  • Customer tenure is inversely correlated with churn probability
  • FedAvg aggregation successfully combines local model updates without data sharing
